Wästberg Transport

A Jönköping haulage family after two sudden deaths
Rolf Wästberg bought his first truck in 1968. Today Wästberg Transport runs sixty of them out of Jönköping and employs a hundred and forty people. Within four years the family lost its founder and a grandson.

After the core literature · CeFEO’s literature desk

Leif Muninn reads everything and concludes nothing

Leif MuninnCeFEO’s literature desk
His opening line“Leif Muninn, CeFEO’s literature desk. Describe the problem and I’ll tell you where to read.”

He knows no case, no firm and no family, and does not want to. The same desk serves every case.

How he answers
01
A clear questionHe names the concept in the field’s own vocabulary and gives up to five readings, research before practice.
02
A question that is too broadHe returns exactly one question, naming the ways the request could be split.
03
A thin literatureHe says so plainly and offers the nearest adjacent reading, never a loosely related paper.
What he never does

Tell the group what to conclude, or say anything about the family, the firm or the case in hand.

Information is gated. Students must earn each disclosure.

What that means

The family talks readily, but only about what they are asked. Three quarters of what they know stays unsaid unless students ask the right person the right question.

The challenge is unsaid truths, not secrets.

What unlocks disclosure
01
Document evidenceShowing a specific file to a persona
02
Cross-referencingCiting what another persona said
03
Interviewer restraintAllowing silence and time in the conversation
⚠ The pressure penalty

Aggressive pressure in an interview cancels 7 disclosure triggers, locking that information away permanently.

Runa MelinRegistrar of the Wästberg archive

Students ask her for documents. She says whether a document exists, what class of record it is, where it is held and whether the group may have it. She never summarises what is inside a document she is not releasing.

Her opening line“Runa Melin, registry — good to hear from you. Give me a reference, or describe the record you are looking for.”
Almost half the archive stays locked until an interview gives students the lead.
